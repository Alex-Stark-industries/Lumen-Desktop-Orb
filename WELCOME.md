# Welcome to Lumen

A step-by-step guide for your very first run — nothing assumed.

## 1. Install

1. Go to the [Releases page](../../releases/latest) and download
   `Lumen Setup <version>.exe`.
2. Double-click it. You'll see an installer window — no administrator
   password is needed, it installs just for your own user account.
3. Pick where to install it (or keep the default) and click through.

**About the SmartScreen warning:** Windows shows a blue *"Windows protected
your PC"* screen for any new app that isn't digitally signed with a paid
certificate — which costs real money every year, so small independent
projects like this one usually skip it. It doesn't mean anything is wrong.
Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## 2. Meet the orb

Once installed, Lumen opens automatically as a small glowing sphere in the
**bottom-right corner** of your screen. It floats above every other window,
always visible.

- **Drag it** anywhere by clicking and holding the left mouse button.
- It gently drifts and pulses on its own — that's normal, it means Lumen is
  running.

## 3. Your first command

**Right-click the orb.** A small dark menu appears, listing every command —
grouped into *info*, *actions*, *launching apps*, and *lookups*.

Try **Time** first: click it, and Lumen speaks the current time out loud,
almost instantly. That's the whole interaction model — right-click, pick a
command, listen.

## 4. Commands that ask you something

A few commands — **Open…**, **Web Search…**, **Weather…** — need one more
piece of information. Selecting one of them opens a small text box asking
exactly what it needs (which app to open, what to search, which city).
Type your answer and press **Enter**.

## 5. The first spoken reply

The very first time Lumen needs to speak, it downloads its voice — a
one-time download of a few hundred MB. You'll see it take a few extra
seconds; every reply after that is instant and works completely offline.

## 6. Closing Lumen

Right-click the orb and choose **Quit Lumen** at the bottom of the menu (in
red). There's no tray icon yet — this is currently the only way to close it.
